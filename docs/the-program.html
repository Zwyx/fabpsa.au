The program

The FABPSA bilingual program integrates French language teaching directly into the Western Australian curriculum — making bilingual education accessible to every family, whether they speak French at home or not. We offer two delivery models, designed to fit any school's readiness and ambition.

The bilingual program​

  • Option 1 – Immersion

Allow gradual exposure and adjustment for students and teachers.

  • Option 2 – The comprehensive program

More immersive with immediate impact on language proficiency.

  • Inclusive and accessible to French and non-French-speakers
  • WA curriculum (SCSA)
  • Language ratio to be adapted based on the school.

Advantages for the school​

  • Enhanced school reputation.
  • Attracts more families.
  • More diverse and inclusive school culture.
  • Opportunity to lead an innovative and forward-thinking educational program.
  • Extra funding from the Department of Education.
  • Potential grants and partnerships.

Advantages for the students​

  • Enhanced cognitive skills.
  • Higher academic achievements (improves results at NAPLAN).
  • Bilingual proficiency improves communication skills.

The services offered by FABPSA​

  • A pool of French-speaking teachers (TRBWA registered) and EA (including special needs).
  • Professional development.
  • The bilingual program itself.
  • Resources.
  • Administration, enrolments.
  • Tutoring.
  • Extracurricular activities.
  • And more...
